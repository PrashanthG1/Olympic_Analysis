In-Depth Analysis of Olympic Athletes (1900-2016)
Project Overview
This project presents an in-depth analysis of Olympic athletes from the years 1900 to 2016, focusing on medal tallies, trends in age and gender, and insights into the performance of athletes in various sports. The analysis includes:

Medal Tally by Country: A comprehensive breakdown of the number of medals earned by countries throughout the years.
Age Group and Gender Analysis: Investigating which age groups and genders are more likely to win medals, and why certain younger athletes dominate in sports like gymnastics.
Team vs. Individual Sports: Analyzing the performance trends in team sports versus individual events, including medal distribution and performance trends.
The project leverages data exploration and visualization techniques to uncover meaningful insights into Olympic performance over the past century.

Libraries Used
The analysis involved the use of several key Python libraries for data manipulation, exploration, and visualization:

Pandas: For data manipulation and cleaning.
NumPy: For numerical computations.
Matplotlib: For creating static, interactive, and animated visualizations.
Seaborn: For generating statistical graphics and enhancing data visualization.
Plotly: For interactive graphs and dashboards.
SciPy: For statistical analysis.
Data Exploration (EDA)
The project began with Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), where we:

Cleaned and pre-processed the data to ensure consistency across the dataset.
Identified key variables such as medal counts, athlete demographics, and country performance across different years.
Investigated trends in age, gender, and performance by analyzing athlete data, including the types of events they participated in.
Explored the distribution of medals among countries and the correlation between age and medal wins, particularly focusing on sports like gymnastics, where younger athletes often excel.
Examined the performance of team sports versus individual events, highlighting which types of sports contribute more to a country's medal tally.
Key Insights
The analysis revealed several important findings:

Age and Gender Trends: Younger athletes, particularly in sports like gymnastics, often outperformed older counterparts due to the physical demands of the sport.
Country Medal Tallies: Some countries consistently ranked high in medal tallies, and these patterns were examined year by year to understand the global sports trends.
Team vs. Individual Sports: The comparison between team sports and individual events highlighted which categories contributed more significantly to medal counts and showcased the differing levels of competition in each category.
Visualization
Several visualizations were created using Matplotlib and Seaborn to represent:

Medal Tally by Country: Heatmaps and bar charts showing the total number of medals won by each country across different years.
Age and Gender Analysis: Distribution plots and scatter plots illustrating the relationship between athlete age, gender, and medal achievement.
Team vs. Individual Sports: Comparative visualizations showcasing the performance of countries in team sports versus individual sports.
